About the store

A small shop with a big point of view

Source Booksellers is an independent bookstore devoted to a unique niche of non-fiction. We believe a focused collection, chosen by people who actually read it, is worth more than endless aisles.

You will find us at 4240 Cass Avenue in Midtown Detroit, open seven days a week, with new titles, pre-orders, and author events on the calendar all year.
